Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use the ShotGain mobile application ("the App").

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and a password. This is used solely for authentication and syncing your data across devices.

Location Data: When you start a GPS round, the App accesses your device's location to show your position on the golf course and calculate distances to the pin. Location data is only collected while you are actively using the GPS Caddie feature. We do not track your location in the background.

Golf Performance Data: The App stores your round scores, shot details (lie type, distance, club used, and where on the course each shot was hit), strokes gained calculations, and practice session data. This data is used to provide you with performance analytics and personalized practice recommendations. When you are signed in, it is synced to your account.

Launch Monitor Data: If you import CSV files from launch monitors (TrackMan, Uneekor, GCQuad, etc.), the shot data from those files is stored to build your club dispersion profiles and improve AI Caddy recommendations.

Launch Monitor Photos: If you are signed in and import a photo or screenshot of a launch-monitor screen instead of a CSV file, the image file is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic's Claude API, which reads the shot numbers in it. The file is sent as it is, including any metadata stored in it; a photo taken with a phone camera can include where it was taken. We do not store the photo; only the shot data you choose to import is saved. We keep a record of each photo import (your user ID, the time, and the AI model used) to apply usage limits.

Usage Analytics: The App records which screens and features you use (for example, starting a round or importing a practice session) and details of that action, such as the course, hole or club, along with the app version, device platform, a session identifier, and an installation identifier that is generated by the App and stored on your device. When you are signed in, these events are linked to your account's user ID. Crash reports are recorded the same way. This data is stored in our own database on Supabase. It is not sent to any analytics or advertising company, and it is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. You can turn it off at any time in the App via Profile → "Crash & Usage Reporting."

Feedback & Course Mapping: If you submit feedback through the App, we collect the message content, feedback type, app version, and platform. If you submit a course mapping request, we collect the course name and GPS coordinates.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's features (GPS tracking, strokes gained analysis, AI Caddy recommendations, practice drills)
  • To sync your data across devices when you are signed in
  • To generate personalized analytics and practice plans based on your performance data
  • To find and fix crashes and understand which features are used, through usage analytics and crash reports (if you have not turned off "Crash & Usage Reporting")

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device and, when you are signed in, synced to our cloud database hosted on Supabase (powered by AWS infrastructure). Data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

We do not serve advertisements and we do not use any advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. Usage analytics (described above) are first-party and stay in our own database. We use a crash and error reporting service (Sentry, described below) to monitor app stability. You can turn off both usage analytics and crash reporting at any time in the App via Profile → "Crash & Usage Reporting." We do not share your golf data with any external analytics or marketing services. Your performance data is yours alone. The third-party services the App uses, and what each one receives, are listed below.

Anonymized club dispersion data (carry distances and lateral spread, with no personally identifiable information) may be used in aggregate to provide cohort comparisons for all users.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — for user authentication and cloud data storage
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim and Overpass API) — for finding golf courses and loading course maps. To find courses near you, a search area around your location is sent; no account information is sent. These requests may be served by public Overpass servers that other organizations run.
  • GolfCourseAPI — for course scorecards (par, yardage and tees). The course being looked up is sent; no account information.
  • Open-Meteo — for wind data during GPS rounds (only the tee coordinates of the hole you are playing are sent; no account information)
  • Google Maps SDK — for rendering course maps on Android devices
  • Apple Maps — for rendering course maps on iOS devices
  • RevenueCat — for subscription management and purchase restoration (only your user ID is shared; no golf data is sent)
  • Sentry — for crash and error reporting to monitor app stability. Sentry receives the device platform, app version, error stack traces, and an installation identifier. When you are signed in, your ShotGain user ID and email are attached to error events so we can correlate reports across sessions. No shot data, location data, or content from your rounds is ever sent to Sentry. You can turn this off at any time in the App via Profile → "Crash & Usage Reporting." Sentry processes data in accordance with their privacy policy at sentry.io/privacy and provides the same level of protection as described in this document.
  • Anthropic — for reading shot data from launch-monitor photos you choose to import (see "Launch Monitor Photos" above). Only the image file is sent, with no account information. Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models, and processes it under its privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Slack — feedback messages and course reports you send are delivered to our team's Slack workspace so we can act on them
  • Expo / EAS — for app delivery and over-the-air updates (no golf data is sent)

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as you maintain an active account. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the App via Profile → Delete Account, or by contacting us. Deleting your account deletes the account and the golf data stored with it (rounds, shots, practice sessions, clubs and dispersion profiles). Feedback messages and course reports you sent, and usage analytics events, may be kept so we can act on them. Local data on your device can be cleared by uninstalling the App.

To save storage on your device, the App automatically removes GPS coordinates from rounds older than 90 days on your local device. Your cloud backup retains the full data. Practice session logs are capped at the 200 most recent entries.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data — available directly in the app via Profile → Delete Account, or by emailing us
  • Withdraw consent for crash reporting and usage analytics at any time via Profile → "Crash & Usage Reporting"
  • Export your data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy within the App and updating the "Last updated" date above.
